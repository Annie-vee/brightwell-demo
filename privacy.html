Privacy Policy

Brightwell Premium Plan Simulation · Last updated 14 November 2026

This website is a classroom demonstration of a fictional product for a fictional training scenario involving Cedar Valley Health. It is not a real hospital system and it is not connected to any hospital.

What we collect

  • When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account. We do not receive your Google password.
  • The app keeps a simple record of your account (email and the demo role you choose) so you stay signed in.
  • Actions you take in the demo, such as uploading a sample certificate file name or running a report, are recorded with your email and the time.

What we do not collect

We do not collect real patient, employee or hospital data. All staff, courses and certificates in the demo are invented. Please do not enter real personal information.

How your information is used

Your information is used only to sign you in, to show you the demo, and to record demo actions. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or use it for anything else.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a Supabase database. The website is hosted on Netlify. Sign-in is provided by Google.

Deleting your data

You can ask for your account and demo activity to be deleted by contacting the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
